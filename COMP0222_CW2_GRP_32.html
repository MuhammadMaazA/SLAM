Why COLMAP for K?
D455 vendor spec fx = 426.7 px. COLMAP recovers f ≈ 421–428 px. Without this step, bundle-adjustment residuals accumulate heading drift proportional to the intrinsic error.
Why correct_scale?
Monocular SLAM has a scale degree of freedom. Umeyama Sim(3) alignment recovers the global scale factor so ATE measures geometric agreement, not scale mismatch.
No external GT
COLMAP SfM trajectory is used as a pseudo-ground-truth. ATE is therefore an inter-method disagreement metric, not absolute accuracy. Named explicitly in all figures.

Q2 · Three Ways Monocular ORB-SLAM2 Breaks
07 / 15
Three failure modes, all observed in our sequences
Failure mode 1
Pure-rotation kills triangulation
No baseline → no parallax → no new map points. Tracking drops to motion-model prior. Seen in BikeStorage2 turning sequences.
Mitigation: maintain translational motion; avoid in-place pivots
Failure mode 2
Featureless walls starve the matcher
Fewer than ~50 ORB inliers → tracking failure. Floor7_Hallway corridor: concrete walls, uniform ceiling. KITTI07 at feat < 1000: RANSAC inlier count never reaches the hardcoded 100-point threshold.
Mitigation: ensure textured scene or increase features
Failure mode 3
Sim(3) scale collapse → 178° flip
Umeyama alignment fits a near-zero scale factor when the trajectory is very short relative to scene depth. The rotation component absorbs this as a 180° flip. OnePoolStreet1 and Entrance2 are both scale-collapse artefacts, not real heading errors.
Mitigation: longer sequences with varied depth
Q2 · Three Takeaways
08 / 15
Q2: Three empirical findings
1
You cannot skip COLMAP intrinsic recovery
D455 vendor f differs up to 10 % from what COLMAP recovers from the actual data. That propagates into drift. Always calibrate from your data, not the spec sheet.
2
Monocular ORB-SLAM2 gives ~1 % drift indoors
Basement_1: 0.036 m / ~3.6 m tracked path. Outdoor degrades 7× — open space means less structured depth, more scale ambiguity in the monocular model.
3
Rotation ATE is a diagnostic, not a failure mode
A 178° rotation error means scale collapsed and Umeyama flipped the trajectory — not a real heading failure. Spotting this saved us from reporting OnePoolStreet1 as a valid result.

Why voxel 0.05 m beats none
Voxel ≥ 0.10 m blurs surface normals below reliable PCA estimation — ICP's point-to-plane residual degrades. 0.05 m preserves detail while reducing search cost.
Why 33 % scan rate fails
Per-step motion grows beyond ICP convergence basin (typically ~0.3 m). Slower acquisition rate means larger motion between consecutive scans.

Summary · Q&A
15 / 15
Five quantified findings
1
Loop closure is load-bearing
KITTI07 ATE: 4.39 m (baseline) → 18 m (no loop). 311 % increase. ORB-SLAM2's Essential Graph correction is the primary drift bound on long loops.
2
Sensor max range is #1 LiDAR parameter
2 000 mm vs 12 000 mm: 12.5× closure error difference. Truncating range removes the far-wall correspondences ICP needs to converge.
3
Feature count is non-monotonic
TUM feat=500 ATE (0.015 m) looks better than baseline (0.032 m), but only 45 % of frames tracked — easy early segment bias. ATE over tracked frames ≠ global accuracy.
4
PGO can degrade when loops are marginal
Basement_1: 0.194 m → 0.239 m after PGO. GTSAM LM hit lambda limit — one loop factor with a slightly mis-estimated transform conflicted with tight odometry. This is a valid scientific result.
5
Both systems degrade the same way
Featureless environments break visual SLAM (no texture) and LiDAR SLAM (no surface normals) for exactly the same geometric reason — insufficient discriminative structure for correspondence matching.
